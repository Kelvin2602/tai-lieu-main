A Conceptual History of Botnets
- Bots originally useful tools for IRC.
- IRC invented Aug 1988 by Jarkko Oikarinen.

Figure 1.1 The Evolution of Bot Technology
Timeline:
- 1988: IRC invented.
- 1989: First Bot (GM).
- 1999: Pretty Park (first worm to use IRC), SubSeven, GT Bot.
- 2002: SDBot (C++ source available), Agobot/Gaobot (modular).
- 2003: Spybot (keylogging), RBot.
- 2004: PolyBot.
- 2005: Mytob (mass mailing).
