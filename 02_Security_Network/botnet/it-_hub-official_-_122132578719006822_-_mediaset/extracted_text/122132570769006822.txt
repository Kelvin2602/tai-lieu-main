Incident Response Criteria
Engage law enforcement if:
- Loss of financial data.
- Loss of student information.
- illegal material (child pornography).

Forensic Usage
- Digital signature of original drive.
- Forensically sound image.
- Chain of custody.
- Quick forensics: extract attack vectors, botnet architecture, code samples.
- Steps:
  1. Receive notification.
  2. Open ticket.
  3. Quarantine network.
  4. Perform quick forensics.
