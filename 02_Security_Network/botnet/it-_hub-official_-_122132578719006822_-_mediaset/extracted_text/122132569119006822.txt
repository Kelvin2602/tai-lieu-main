Jeffrey Parson Sentencing
Parson was sentenced to 18 months in jail, three years of supervised release.
Judge Pechman considered his unique circumstances: he was just past his 18th birthday, history of mental illness.
Conditions: No video games, no chat rooms. Only educational and business computer use.
