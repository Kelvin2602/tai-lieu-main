Antimalware Terminology
- "Antivirus" implies only viruses, but modern suites detect worms, Trojans, bots, spyware, phishing.
- "Antimalware" might be a better term.
- Detection is sophisticated but rarely 100% for all malware types.

Antivirus Signatures
- "Search string" is technically more accurate.
- "Signature" has other meanings (digital sigs).
