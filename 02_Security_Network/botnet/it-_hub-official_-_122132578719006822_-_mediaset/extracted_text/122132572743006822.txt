Phishing Life Cycle (continued)
- Attack: Launches attack (Web, Email, IM, etc.).
- Collection: Collects credentials (web form, email response).
- Fraud: Cashers convert credentials to cash/goods.
- Post Attack: Shutdown, erase evidence, launder proceeds.

Storage and Distribution of Illegal content
- Botnets used for storage of warez, movies, games.
- Hidden directories (Recycle Bin with special SID-like names).
- Losses from piracy ($2.3 billion in 2005).
