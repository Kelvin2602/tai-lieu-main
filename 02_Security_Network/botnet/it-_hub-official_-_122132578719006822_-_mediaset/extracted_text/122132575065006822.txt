Malware in the Wild (WildList)

- Reported by 80+ AV professionals.
- Monthly list (often delayed).
- Used by AV testing orgs (Virus Bulletin, ICSA Labs).
- Sept 2006: 400 variants of W32/Sdbot alone.
- Primary List vs Secondary List (B-list malware).
