Layer 2 Attacks
- ARP Spoofing/Poisoning: Attacker associates their MAC with Victim IP or Router IP.
  - Enables Man-in-the-Middle (MITM).
- Switch Forwarding Table Overflow (CAM Table Overflow):
  - Floods switch with fake MACs.
  - Switch fails open (hub mode), broadcasting all traffic.
  - Tools: Ettercap.
