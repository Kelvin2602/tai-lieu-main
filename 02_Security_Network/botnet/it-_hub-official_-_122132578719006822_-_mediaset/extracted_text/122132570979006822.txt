Bot Classifications and Naming
- Mass mailing bot based on MyDoom.
- Antivirus vendors tag components (e.g., Hacktool.HideWindow, Trojan.Dropper).
- Overall bot might be RBot, but AV sees parts.
- Mismatch in naming between vendors.
- Variants have different capabilities (IRC vs P2P, keylogging vs financial theft).

Spybot
Open source Trojan, derivative of SDBot. Also called Milkit.
Adds spyware capabilities (logging, form grabbing).
