Spybot Infection Clues

System Folder
Spybot places a copy of itself in %System% (e.g. C:\Windows\System32).
Common filenames:
- Bling.exe
- Netwmon.exe
- Wuamgrd.exe

Registry Entries
- Value: "dir0" = "012345:[CONFIGURABLE PATH]" in HKEY_CURRENT_USER\SOFTWARE\KAZAA\LocalContent
- Value: "Microsoft Update" = "wuamgrd.exe" in HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run
