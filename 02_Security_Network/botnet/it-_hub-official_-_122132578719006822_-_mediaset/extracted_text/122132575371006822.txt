Phishing Economy & Cashing Out
- "The economy of phishing" (Abad, Firstmonday.org).
- Roles: Botherders, Phishers, Cashers.
- Cashers buy credentials (commission 50-70%).
- Full credentials (high balance) = up to $100.
- Cashing out: Duplicate ATM cards, Western Union.
- Adware Rates: Dollar-Revenue (30 cents US), GimmyCash (40 cents US).
